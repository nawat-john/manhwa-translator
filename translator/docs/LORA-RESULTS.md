# Korean dialogue LoRA: first experiment, 2026-10-22

The selected adapter improves agreement with held-out synthetic translations,
but loses the blind comparison on the small real OCR sample. It still produces
meaning errors and unnatural dialogue. It is an
experimental checkpoint; the extension's active model is unchanged.

Training used 5,000 synthetic Korean-English pairs, with a separate 500-pair
validation split. Rank-16 query/value LoRA trained 2,359,296 parameters for
three epochs on the RTX 3050 Laptop GPU (4 GB). Training took 841 seconds
(14 minutes). Validation token loss fell from 2.3557 to 1.5833, 1.5105 and
1.4565; epoch three was selected before evaluating the test split.
The saved adapter is 9,457,520 bytes; it requires the existing trimmed base.

## Held-out translation quality and normal latency

All 500 test pairs were translated with five beams in FP32. BLEU and chrF++
are higher-is-better corpus scores, **not percentages of correct translations**.

| Model | BLEU | chrF++ | Median ms/input | Mean ms/input | p95 ms/input |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Original M2M100 | 10.48 | 30.50 | 324.8 | 340.0 | 474.9 |
| Trimmed baseline | 10.51 | 30.59 | 301.6 | 316.0 | 443.7 |
| Trimmed + merged LoRA | 21.90 | 42.34 | 381.0 | 396.6 | 565.3 |

The adapter adds 11.39 BLEU and 11.76 chrF++ over the trimmed baseline.
Normal translation median latency increases by **26.3%** over the trimmed
baseline (+79.4 ms/input), or **17.3%** over the original untrimmed model.
Output averages 9.65 whitespace-separated words versus 8.03 for the trimmed
baseline, so the models are doing different amounts of autoregressive work.
This is a measured association, not proof that output length explains all
of the slowdown.

With exactly 32 new tokens, five beams, 20 identical inputs and three timed
passes after warm-up, the trimmed baseline median is 684.2 ms and merged
LoRA is 723.8 ms: **5.8% slower in this run**. Both use the same architecture
after merging. These measurements do not establish a universal speed penalty;
GPU clocks, beam contents and run order can affect small differences.

Timing excludes loading, OCR, inpainting and rendering. Normal translation
timing includes tokenization and decoding; fixed-length timing measures
generation only. These numbers therefore do not predict full-page latency.

## Limits and concrete examples

The independent MiniMax judge received Korean sources and shuffled A/B
candidates without model names or reference answers:

| Sample | LoRA preferred | Trimmed baseline preferred | Tie |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 100 held-out synthetic test pairs | 76 | 22 | 2 |
| 24 actual webtoon OCR texts | 7 | 12 | 5 |

**Recommendation: keep the current extension baseline.** The synthetic-domain
gain does not carry over to this small, noisy real OCR sample. This is not
enough evidence to claim regression on every webtoon, but it does not justify
replacing the active model. Before another training experiment, add a separate
evaluation set of correctly transcribed webtoon-like Korean dialogue, and
include fragmented/noisy OCR cases with conservative translation targets.
Keep these existing test results fixed rather than selecting another checkpoint
against the same test set.

On the existing OCR sample, `맞아요` changes from `right is.` to `It's right.`,
and `마술사?` changes from `The wizard?` to `The magician?`.
However, another OCR line changes from `I am a wizard in society.` to
`I'm really a magist in society.`: the adapter can introduce bad English too.
Some OCR fragments are already incomplete, and translation cannot reliably
reconstruct missing text.

The test references are synthetic, generated by the same teacher family as
training. Higher scores demonstrate better agreement on this distribution,
not general accuracy on Korean webtoons. Blind A/B judgments are relative
preferences; choosing one poor translation over another does not make it
correct. No Korean human reviewer has checked this experiment.

See [LORA.md](LORA.md) for reproducible commands and evaluation protocol.
Local weights live in `models/ko-en-dialogue-lora-v1/best/` and are excluded
from Git. Raw numerical results and blind verdicts are archived in
`reports/lora-v1/`.
